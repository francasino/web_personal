Level 01 — Profile

The researcher

Dr. Fran Casino

Ramón y Cajal Senior Researcher & Lecturer

Universitat Rovira i Virgili (URV) · Departament d'Enginyeria Informàtica i Matemàtiques (DEIM) · ASCLEPIUS Research Group

Find me

    Dr. Fran Casino (Francisco José Casino Cembellín, Tarragona, 1986) is a member of the teaching and research staff in the Department of Computer Engineering and Mathematics (DEIM) at Universitat Rovira i Virgili (URV), and a researcher in the ASCLEPIUS research group.

    His research focuses on cybersecurity, digital forensics, blockchain, cognitive security and privacy-enhancing technologies. Since 2021 he has been included annually in Stanford University's World's Top 2% Scientists ranking, and in 2026 entered the career-long ranking. In 2024 he was elevated to IEEE Senior Member.

    He was awarded the Ramón y Cajal Senior Fellowship by the Spanish Ministry of Science, Innovation and Universities (RYC2021-031024-I), and won the Premi Joan Pedro i Gallego 2025 at URV for social impact in research.
